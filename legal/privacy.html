CleanSwipe analyzes photos on your device and does not upload photo or video content. Apple manages iCloud Photos sync if you enable it. CleanSwipe has no separate backend account system. It includes the Meta iOS SDK for optional install measurement. Meta measurement runs only after you grant App Tracking Transparency permission. The app also uses Firebase Analytics Core and Firebase Crashlytics for usage and diagnostics, and RevenueCat for subscription entitlements.

Photo Library

#

CleanSwipe scans photos and videos locally on the user's device through Apple's PhotoKit framework. CleanSwipe does not upload photo or video content. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple syncs the Photos library and deletion can sync to other devices. CleanSwipe may save local-only analysis indexes, review snapshots, and generated thumbnails on the device to make future scans faster. CleanSwipe manages this local analysis data and resets it when photo access changes. This does not delete anything from Photos.

People Groups

#

If the bundled SFace Core ML model passes its local startup check and the user starts a People Scan, CleanSwipe creates anonymous 128-dimensional face embeddings and groups only on that iPhone. It does not import names or group assignments from Apple Photos, does not upload faces, crops, embeddings, identifiers, or group membership, and does not keep separate copies of photos. Release diagnostics keep only aggregate processing-stage counters and never include photos, crops, embeddings, memberships, or identifiers. Clearing CleanSwipe's local analysis removes the derived embeddings and groups without deleting photos.

Photo Map

#

The Library map groups photos and videos using location metadata already saved in each item. CleanSwipe keeps this index on the device and does not send photo coordinates or identifiers to CleanSwipe analytics or servers. Apple Maps provides the map and may receive the visible map area and map interactions needed to display it. CleanSwipe does not request the user's current location. Clearing CleanSwipe's local analysis removes the saved map index.

Home Place Names

#

CleanSwipe manages place names for Home suggestions. When a trip or place suggestion appears on Home, CleanSwipe may ask Apple's reverse geocoder for a city name using only the center of an approximate 5 km area. It does not send an individual photo's coordinates, identifier, or date. Requests are rate-limited and names are cached on this device. CleanSwipe resets cached place names when local photo access is reset.

Clutter Suggestions

#

CleanSwipe checks photos and videos on the device for possible receipts and documents, accidental shots, and very short videos. These are only suggestions. CleanSwipe does not upload the images or automatically delete suggested items.

Cleanup Control

#

Swipe decisions only create a cleanup list. Photos and videos are not deleted until the user reviews the summary and confirms deletion through the system Photos confirmation flow.

AI Best Shot

#

AI Best Shot recommendations are generated locally from photo quality signals such as focus, lighting, contrast, and composition. AI recommends likely keepers but does not delete photos automatically.

Purchases

#

Premium entitlement is handled through RevenueCat and App Store purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase history for app functionality. CleanSwipe does not store payment information or process payment card data.

Optional Meta Install Measurement

#

Meta measurement is off unless you allow tracking in iOS. Before you grant permission, CleanSwipe does not initialize the Meta SDK or send app events to Meta. After permission, the SDK sends app activation and session events and device identifiers to Meta for advertising measurement. The SDK may also process limited, non-linked app analytics and crash diagnostics after initialization. If you later revoke permission, CleanSwipe disables ad ID collection on your next return if the SDK is already running. On a fresh launch without permission, the SDK remains uninitialized, and CleanSwipe sends no app activation event. CleanSwipe does not send photos, videos, filenames, local photo identifiers, photo locations, face embeddings, scan results, or cleanup decisions to Meta. You can change this permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ad measurement is optional and is not needed to use CleanSwipe.

Data Collection

#

Firebase may collect app usage, device/app, and diagnostic/crash data for Analytics and Crashlytics. Firebase events are limited to screen/action names and aggregate counts or categories, such as scan totals, review scope, cleanup category, and cleanup outcome counts. CleanSwipe does not send photo or video content, filenames, local photo identifiers, thumbnails, raw scan output, item-level cleanup decisions, purchase receipts, payment information, or precise location or photo coordinates to Firebase. RevenueCat may process purchase history for Premium entitlement. Apple Maps may receive the visible map area and map interactions to render the Library map, and the approximate Home suggestion area when Place Names is enabled.

Your Choices

#

You can limit or revoke Photos access in iOS Settings. CleanSwipe resets local analysis when photo access changes without deleting items from Photos. You can change ad measurement permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can manage subscriptions through your Apple account.

Contact

#

For privacy or support questions, use the support link listed on CleanSwipe's App Store product page.
