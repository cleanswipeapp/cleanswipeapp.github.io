Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

CleanSwipe is designed to clean up a photo library without uploading the user's photos. The current build has no backend account system, analytics SDK, ad SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or cloud sync.

Photo Library

CleanSwipe scans photos and videos locally on the user's device through Apple's PhotoKit framework. Photo and video content is not uploaded by CleanSwipe in this build.

Cleanup Control

Swipe decisions only create a cleanup list. Photos and videos are not deleted until the user reviews the summary and confirms deletion through the system Photos confirmation flow.

AI Best Shot

AI Best Shot recommendations are generated locally from photo quality signals such as focus, lighting, contrast, and composition. AI recommends likely keepers but does not delete photos automatically.

Purchases

Premium entitlement is handled by StoreKit and the App Store. CleanSwipe does not run a purchase backend, collect receipts, store payment information, or process payment card data.

Data Collection

This build does not collect user, device, usage, diagnostic, photo, video, purchase receipt, or location data on a CleanSwipe server or third-party analytics service.

App Store Privacy Label

For the current no-backend build, CleanSwipe's App Store privacy answers should state that no data is collected by the app. If analytics, crash-reporting, accounts, sync, support forms, or any backend service are added later, this policy and App Store Connect privacy answers must be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support link listed on CleanSwipe's App Store product page.
