CleanSwipe Premium offers weekly, monthly, and yearly auto-renewable subscription plans. A 3-day free trial may be available to eligible users. A subscription renews for its selected billing period until cancelled in App Store account settings.

Subscription

#

Before starting a subscription, you can use CleanSwipe's on-device scanning, review groups, cleanup summary, confirmed deletion, and AI Best Shot recommendations. After a previously activated trial or paid period expires, access requires a new active subscription or restoration of an existing entitlement. The subscription automatically renews unless it is cancelled before the end of the current billing period. Pricing is shown in the app before purchase and may vary by country or region.

Premium Features

#

An active trial or subscription keeps CleanSwipe's local photo cleanup tools available. AI Best Shot recommends likely keepers but never deletes photos automatically. The user remains responsible for every keep, cleanup, and final deletion decision.

Cancellation and Restore

#

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Users can cancel, restore, or manage renewal in App Store account settings. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

Photo Cleanup

#

CleanSwipe provides local review tools and estimates. Cleanup results, storage estimates, and AI recommendations may not be perfect. Users should review the final cleanup summary before confirming deletion.

Standard EULA

#

CleanSwipe uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement unless a custom EULA is later provided in App Store Connect. View Apple's Standard EULA.

Privacy

#

CleanSwipe's privacy practices are described in the Privacy Policy.

Contact

#

For support questions, use the support link listed on CleanSwipe's App Store product page.
