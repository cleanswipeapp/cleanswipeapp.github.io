FAQ

Good questions.

Will CleanSwipe delete my photos automatically?

No. Swiping only builds a cleanup plan. CleanSwipe moves photos only after you review the summary and confirm, and iOS shows its own Photos confirmation too.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. CleanSwipe scans and groups your library on your iPhone and doesn't upload photo or video content. See the Privacy Policy for details on app diagnostics.

Can I get a photo back after cleanup?

Yes. Removed items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for the period iOS keeps them there.

What about iCloud Photos?

If iCloud Photos is turned on, Apple syncs your library — so items you remove will also be removed from your other devices. CleanSwipe reminds you of this before you confirm.

Is CleanSwipe free?

CleanSwipe is free to download. An optional Premium subscription is available in the app; you can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
